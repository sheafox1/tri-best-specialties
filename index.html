Our Story

Built on quality, honesty and commitment

Tri Best Specialties was founded by Anthony Ilnicky, a licensed carpenter and trade specialist who followed his father into the trade and joined the United Brotherhood of Carpenters. After moving to Dallas-Fort Worth from California, he started the company on three principles: quality work, honesty and commitment.
